# Policy wording (2025 editions)
Source: `policy_clauses` table in `data/claims.sqlite`. Authoritative for every cover, limit, excess, exclusion and condition decision. Where this file and the database disagree, the database wins.

## Home

### H-1.1 — Period of insurance

*Type:* Cover · *Section:* General

We cover loss or damage that happens during the period of insurance shown on your schedule. When you renew, the new period follows the last one without a break. Loss or damage that happened in an earlier period is covered under the terms in force at that time. We do not cover anything that happened before you first took out this policy.

### H-1.2 — Excess

*Type:* Excess · *Section:* General

For each claim you pay the excess shown on your schedule. A claim for escape of water carries the escape of water excess instead of the standard excess. A claim for subsidence carries an excess of £1,000. If one incident leads to a claim under both the Buildings and the Contents sections, the excess applies to each section.

### H-2.1 — Insured events

*Type:* Cover · *Section:* Buildings

We cover your buildings against loss or damage caused by fire, lightning, explosion or smoke; storm; flood; escape of water or oil from a fixed installation; theft or attempted theft; subsidence, heave or landslip; impact; and malicious damage. Accidental damage is covered only if shown on your schedule.

### H-2.2 — Escape of water

*Type:* Cover · *Section:* Buildings

We cover damage caused by water escaping suddenly from a fixed water or heating installation, including the cost of finding the leak and making good (trace and access) up to £5,000. We do not cover the cost of repairing the part that failed.

### H-2.3 — Storm

*Type:* Cover · *Section:* Buildings

Storm means strong winds with gusts of at least 47 mph, heavy rain of at least 25 mm in an hour, or snow or hail heavy enough to damage hard surfaces. We do not cover damage to fences, gates or hedges caused by storm.

### H-3.1 — Contents

*Type:* Cover · *Section:* Contents

We cover your contents in the home against the events in clause H-2.1. Accidental damage to contents is covered only if shown on your schedule.

### H-3.2 — Valuables

*Type:* Limit · *Section:* Contents

Valuables are jewellery, watches, items of gold, silver or other precious metal, works of art and collections. For any one valuable we pay up to the single article limit on your schedule, unless the item is listed on your schedule as a specified item, when we pay up to its specified value. Valuables in total are limited to one third of the contents sum insured.

### H-4.1 — Most we pay

*Type:* Limit · *Section:* General

The most we pay for one claim under a section is the sum insured for that section shown on your schedule, less the excess.

### H-5.1 — Wear and tear and gradual damage

*Type:* Exclusion · *Section:* Exclusions

We do not cover loss or damage caused by wear and tear or gradual deterioration, or by anything that happens gradually, such as water seeping over time through a shower seal or from a slow leak.

### H-5.2 — Unoccupied home

*Type:* Exclusion · *Section:* Exclusions

We do not cover theft, escape of water or malicious damage once the home has been unoccupied for more than 60 days in a row.

### H-6.1 — Reporting theft

*Type:* Condition · *Section:* Claims conditions

You must report theft, attempted theft or malicious damage to the police as soon as possible and give us the crime reference number.

### H-6.2 — Proof of ownership and value

*Type:* Condition · *Section:* Claims conditions

For any item over £500 you must give us proof of ownership and value. This can be a receipt, a bank or card statement, a photograph showing the item, the original packaging or manual, or a valuation.

### H-6.3 — How we settle

*Type:* Condition · *Section:* Claims conditions

We may repair, replace or pay cash. Where we can repair or replace through our suppliers, we may pay the supplier directly. We may make an interim payment before the claim is settled.

## Motor

### M-1.1 — Period of insurance

*Type:* Cover · *Section:* General

We cover loss or damage that happens during the period of insurance shown on your schedule. When you renew, the new period follows the last one without a break. We do not cover anything that happened before you first took out this policy.

### M-1.2 — Excess

*Type:* Excess · *Section:* General

For each claim for damage to or theft of your car you pay the compulsory excess and any voluntary excess shown on your schedule. A windscreen repair carries no excess; a windscreen replacement carries the windscreen excess instead. If the accident was not your fault and we recover our costs in full from the other driver's insurer, we refund your excess.

### M-2.1 — Damage to your car

*Type:* Cover · *Section:* Your car

Under Comprehensive cover we pay to repair your car after accidental damage, fire, theft or malicious damage. If it cannot be repaired economically, we pay its market value (a total loss) and take the salvage.

### M-2.2 — Fire and theft

*Type:* Cover · *Section:* Your car

Under Third party, fire and theft cover we pay for loss of or damage to your car caused by fire, theft or attempted theft only.

### M-2.3 — Windscreen

*Type:* Cover · *Section:* Your car

Under Comprehensive cover we pay to repair or replace broken glass in the windscreen and windows through our windscreen supplier, including recalibrating driver-assistance cameras.

### M-3.1 — Most we pay

*Type:* Limit · *Section:* General

The most we pay for loss of or damage to your car is its market value at the time of the loss, less the excess.

### M-4.1 — Keys and security

*Type:* Exclusion · *Section:* Exclusions

We do not cover theft of your car, or of anything from it, if the keys were left in or on the car, or it was left unlocked or with the engine running while unattended.

### M-4.2 — Drivers

*Type:* Exclusion · *Section:* Exclusions

We do not cover the car while it is driven by anyone not named on your certificate of motor insurance.

### M-5.1 — Reporting theft and evidence

*Type:* Condition · *Section:* Claims conditions

You must report theft or malicious damage to the police as soon as possible and give us the crime reference number. For a total loss you must send us the logbook (V5C) and all keys.
