# Exception families

Derived from the procedures (R-01 to R-08) and the policy wording only, not from the answer key. Version 2. Each family has a test and a legitimate counterpart: the case that looks like the exception but is allowed. Checking the counterpart is what keeps an agent from wrongly flagging good claims.

| Family | What goes wrong | Test | Legitimate counterpart | Rule, clauses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 Cover period | Incident outside cover | incident_date < first_incepted, or after cover_end | Incident in an earlier period of a renewed policy is covered under that period | R-01; H-1.1, M-1.1 |
| F2 Limits | Settlement above a limit | Item or section above single article limit, specified value, sum insured or market value; trace and access over £5,000 | A valuable listed as a specified item is covered up to its specified value, above the single article limit | R-02; H-2.2, H-3.2, H-4.1, M-3.1 |
| F3 Excess | Excess taken wrongly | Excess deducted across payments exceeds recorded excess; wrong excess type; refund missing on a fully recovered non-fault claim | A claim under both Buildings and Contents carries an excess for each section; a windscreen repair carries none | R-03; H-1.2, M-1.2 |
| F4 Evidence | Settled without required evidence | Settlement proposed or paid while evidence incomplete; theft or malicious damage with no crime reference; item over £500 with no proof | Proof of ownership can be a card statement, photograph, packaging or valuation, not only a receipt | R-04; H-6.1, H-6.2, M-5.1 |
| F5 Supplier invoices | Same work charged twice | Same supplier with matching amounts, or overlapping service dates, on different claims | Same supplier working on different properties on the same day, with distinct work | R-05 |
| F6 Declines | Decline not supported | Decline clause does not apply to the facts, or the evidence contradicts it; approved below Team Leader | Decline under a clause the supplier's report supports | R-06; H-5.1, H-5.2, M-4.1, M-4.2 |
| F7 Authority | Settlement approved without authority | Approver's settlement authority below the settlement | Provisional settlement not yet approved | R-07 |
| F8 Complaints | Complaint handled outside DISP, or its outcome not supported by the wording and evidence | Summary resolution after 3 business days; final response after 8 weeks; redress paid late; outcome relies on cover, a clause or a fact that the policy schedule, wording or claim records contradict | Complaint still within its time limit; complaint upheld on service grounds (delay, communication) without relying on cover the policy lacks | R-08 |
