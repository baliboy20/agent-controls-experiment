# Glossary

| Term | Meaning in this dataset |
|---|---|
| Agreed amount | What the handler agreed to pay for an item (`claim_items.agreed_amount_gbp`) |
| Excess | The part of a claim the policyholder pays; recorded per claim in `claims.excess_gbp` |
| First incepted | Date the policy was first taken out; cover has run without a break since |
| Interim payment | A payment made before the claim is settled |
| Legitimate counterpart | A record that looks like an exception but is allowed by the wording |
| Market value | The car's value at the time of loss; the motor limit (M-3.1) |
| Proposed settlement | Agreed items less excess; provisional until approved (R-07) |
| Reserve | What is still expected to be paid on a claim |
| Section | Buildings, Contents or Motor; limits and excesses apply per section |
| Single article limit | Most paid for one unspecified valuable (H-3.2) |
| Specified item | A valuable listed on the schedule with its own value (H-3.2) |
| Sum insured | Most paid under a section (H-4.1) |
| Trace and access | Finding a leak and making good; capped at £5,000 (H-2.2) |
